BRUTA

PHOTOGRAPHIC ARCHIVE OF MY BRUTA PROJECT, A TYPOGRAPHY AND PHOTOBOOK BASED ON MADRID'S BRUTALIST ARCHITECTURE.

Antigua Embajada Británica

BRUTA

THE CENTRAL CONCEPT OF THE BRUTA PROJECT IS TO APPLY THE CHARACTERISTICS AND CONSTRUCTIVE LOGIC OF BÉTON BRUT TO GRAPHIC DESIGN, SPECIFICALLY TO TYPOGRAPHY. THE FUNDAMENTAL BASIS IS TO UNDERSTAND TYPOGRAPHY AS A STRUCTURE CAPABLE OF TRANSLATING THE MONUMENTALITY AND WEIGHT OF MADRID'S BRUTALIST ARCHITECTURE INTO GRAPHIC DESIGN.

THE BRUTA NORMAL VERSION IS CONCEIVED AS THE FUNCTIONAL CORE OF THE TYPOGRAPHIC PROJECT. IT PROPOSES A LESS LITERAL INTERPRETATION OF THE ARCHITECTURAL STYLE, SEEKING A PERFECT BALANCE BETWEEN THE VISUAL FORCEFULNESS OF BRUTALISM AND THE CONVENTIONS OF TRADITIONAL TYPOGRAPHIC DESIGN. ITS STRUCTURE MAINTAINS THE HEAVY WEIGHT AND THICK VOLUMES CHARACTERISTIC OF CONCRETE BUT PRIORITIZES HIGH LEGIBILITY. THIS TECHNICAL ADAPTABILITY ALLOWS THE TEXT TO FUNCTION AND BE USABLE IN ANY FORMAT AND MEDIUM, BOTH ON A LARGE SCALE AND IN SMALL BODY TEXT.

MORPHOLOGICALLY, THIS VARIANT SOFTENS THE RIGIDITY OF THE SYSTEM BY INTRODUCING CERTAIN CURVES AND MORE ORGANIC SHAPES INTO THE ANATOMY OF THE CHARACTERS. THIS DESIGN DECISION WORKS AS A DIRECT NOD TO THE PLASTICITY OF MADRID LANDMARKS LIKE TORRES BLANCAS, WHERE THE HEAVY MATERIAL YIELDS TO THE FLUIDITY OF THE CURVE. IN THIS SENSE, THE DESIGN OF ITS COUNTERS EMULATES THE INTERPLAY OF VOIDS AND SOLIDS CHARACTERISTIC OF THESE FACADES. THE INTERNAL WHITES ACT AS ARCHITECTURAL SPACES THAT BREATHE WITHIN THE TEXT BLOCK, CONSOLIDATING BRUTA NORMAL AS A CONTEMPORARY TYPOGRAPHIC TOOL WITH ITS OWN VOICE.

THE BRUTA RECTA VERSION PROPOSES A DIRECT AND MUCH MORE LITERAL FORMAL TRANSLATION OF MADRID'S BRUTALIST ARCHITECTURE. IT DISPENSES WITH ANY VISUAL ORNAMENTATION TO PUSH THE CONSTRUCTIVE PARAMETERS OF THESE LOCAL BUILDINGS TO THEIR STRICTEST AND MOST RADICAL LIMIT. DUE TO ITS HARSHNESS AND GEOMETRY, IT WORKS BEST AS A DISPLAY TYPEFACE. ITS UNORNAMENTED STRUCTURE IS DESIGNED TO GENERATE GREAT VISUAL IMPACT AND DOMINATE LARGE FORMATS.

MORPHOLOGICALLY, IT IS BUILT EXCLUSIVELY USING STRAIGHT LINES AND ORTHOGONAL CUTS THAT EMULATE THE RIGIDITY OF THIS ARCHITECTURAL STYLE. THIS GEOMETRIC DECISION FAITHFULLY REFLECTS THE MODULARITY AND REPETITIVE GRIDS PRESENT IN EMBLEMATIC MADRID FACADES, SUCH AS THE IBM BUILDING, AMONG OTHERS. CONSEQUENTLY, ITS COUNTERS AND INTERIOR SPACES ARE RESOLVED THROUGH STRICTLY SQUARE OR RECTANGULAR SHAPES. THIS CONFIGURATION GIVES THE TYPEFACE A HIGH VISUAL DENSITY, REPLICATING ON THE GRAPHIC MEDIUM THE MASSIVENESS, STRUCTURAL WEIGHT, AND PHYSICAL PRESENCE CHARACTERISTIC OF MADRID'S BRUTALIST BUILDINGS.
